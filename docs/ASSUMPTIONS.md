# Assumptions

- Exact building year 1990 remains ambiguous and produces manual review until clarified.
- Exact building year 2010 is acceptable but not target because the guide says “newer than 2010.”
- Exact five-year loss value of $100,000 remains ambiguous and produces manual review until clarified.
- Exactly 50% acceptable construction remains ambiguous and produces manual review until the weighting basis is clarified.
- TIV and premium range endpoints are treated as inclusive configuration because they are written as ranges; this interpretation remains visible rather than being attributed verbatim to Federato.
- Premium must represent total premium for the submission; values with an unknown monthly, quarterly, or annual cadence require clarification before comparison.
- Loss valuation basis, five-year date anchor, duplicate treatment, construction weighting, multi-building aggregation, and primary-state derivation remain unresolved.
- Cached fixtures are synthetic and must never be presented as live Federato data.
